# Shape Duplication

You can find this feature in <mark style="color:yellow;">Shape Stats</mark> tab of your shape dashboard.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (155).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### How to duplicate my shape?

* Go to the <mark style="color:yellow;">Shape Stats</mark> and scroll down until you find **Duplicate This Shape** section.
* You simply need to put a new name for the duplicated shape and click on Create Shape button.
* You will need to create a new bot and provide its bot token after.
*   Check out: [Getting a Discord Bot Token](getting-a-discord-bot-token.md) for the steps to get a token.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (156).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



### Notes

* This will not create a _complete_ copy of the shape.
* It will only create a duplicated shape with same Personality and Engine sections configurations (Free Will, Image, Voice, AI Engine) and custom messages.
* Knowledge and Training section won't be duplicated.
* Analytics (User, Server and Message count) won't be duplicated as well.
